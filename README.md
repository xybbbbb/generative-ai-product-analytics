# Generative AI Product Analytics

## Project Overview

This is an independent research project investigating user behavior, product experience, engagement, retention, and monetization across major generative AI products.

The study focuses on four representative products:

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Doubao
- DeepSeek

The project combines competitive analysis, user surveys, behavioral research, statistical analysis, machine learning, and product strategy.

---

## Research Objectives

The project aims to answer four major questions:

1. How do user behaviors and use cases differ across generative AI products?
2. What factors influence user engagement and continued usage?
3. What factors are associated with users' willingness to pay for AI products?
4. Can AI users be segmented into distinct user groups based on their behaviors and needs?

---

## Project Structure

### Part 0 — Competitive Landscape

Comparative analysis of ChatGPT, Gemini, Doubao, and DeepSeek.

### Part 1 — User Research

Design and implementation of user surveys and behavioral research.

### Part 2 — User Behavior Analysis

Analysis of usage patterns, use cases, frequency, and engagement.

### Part 3 — User Experience

Analysis of satisfaction, perceived value, and product preferences.

### Part 4 — Engagement & Retention

Investigation of factors associated with continued AI product usage.

### Part 5 — Monetization

Analysis of willingness to pay and potential drivers of subscription behavior.

### Part 6 — User Segmentation

Clustering users based on their behaviors, needs, and product preferences.

### Part 7 — Business & Product Strategy

Development of data-driven recommendations for AI product positioning, retention, and monetization.

---

## Research Hypotheses

### H1
ChatGPT users may demonstrate higher cross-scenario usage depth.

### H2
Gemini usage may be influenced by users' dependence on the Google ecosystem.

### H3
Doubao users may demonstrate more diverse and everyday-oriented usage patterns.

### H4
DeepSeek users may show stronger preferences for technical, programming, and complex reasoning tasks.

### H5
Generative AI users may frequently use multiple AI products rather than relying on a single product.
